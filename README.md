## 4. STYLE / DESIGN INSTRUCTIONS




**General tone:** professional, calm, human, trustworthy.  
**Style inspiration:** mix the clarity of Be Life Medical Clinic with the warmth of a family practice.




### Colors & typography
- Base colors: white, light grey, soft beige or blue accents.  
- Avoid bright cosmetic tones.  
- Use rounded edges, ample white space, and modern sans-serif fonts (e.g., Lato, Open Sans, or Nunito).  
- Ensure high contrast for accessibility.




### Imagery
- Realistic medical and clinic photos: building facade, consultation rooms, team portraits.  
- For the aesthetic page: neutral and elegant visuals (focus on skin texture and confidence, not beauty shots or injections).  
- Consistent lighting and style across photos.




### Layout
- Sticky header menu for easy navigation.  
- Clean sections with clear titles and soft animations (fade-in on scroll).  
- Footer on all pages with contact details, language switch, privacy policy link and © Cabinet Mutsaard 2025.




### Multilingual setup
- Full content available in Dutch, French and English.  
- Each page and menu title should exist in all three languages.  
- Language switch clearly visible (top right corner and footer).
